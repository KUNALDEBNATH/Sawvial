How to use SAWVIAL

Four steps, about thirty seconds.

01

Choose your language

Pick the language you are most comfortable speaking.

02

Speak or type your question

Tap the microphone and talk the way you would to a person.

03

Read the answer below your question

The reply appears directly under what you asked.

04

Listen, copy or come back later

Tap Listen to have the answer read aloud.

What can I ask about?

Crops, soil, water, pests and general farm practice.

Does it work without typing?

Yes. The microphone is the main way in.

Where are my questions stored?

In your own browser on this device.

Should I follow the advice exactly?

Treat it as general, research-based guidance.
